How it works

One row per person. One page for the team.

Nobody fills in a report. The board is the report, and it is always this week.

  1. 01
    Each person writes their own row on Monday.

    Three to five tasks across the week. It takes about two minutes, and nobody else can edit it without leaving a note.

  2. 02
    A task changes colour as it moves.

    In progress, done, waiting on someone, or slipped. A slipped task moves to the next day on its own and keeps its red mark, so the team sees it without asking.

  3. 03
    Friday closes the week.

    What was done stays as a record. What was not done starts next week's row, already filled in.

# Code of Conduct

## Our promise

We want handmade to be a friendly place to learn and build. Everyone is welcome, whatever their experience, background, identity or level of communication.

## What we expect

- Be kind and patient, above all with people who are new.
- Criticize code and ideas, never people.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.
- Accept feedback and give it in a way you would like to receive it.
- Credit other people's work.

## What is not acceptable

- Insults, harassment or personal attacks.
- Sexual language or imagery and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Discrimination or jokes about any group of people.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Repeatedly pushing a point after you have been asked to stop.

## Where this applies

In the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and any space where you represent the project.

## Reporting

Email the maintainer at **h.hiramkarogo@gmail.com** or use GitHub's private "Report content" option. Reports are read only by the maintainer and kept private.

## What happens next

The maintainer will look at every report and may:

1. Talk to the person privately and ask them to change.
2. Give a public warning.
3. Remove comments or code.
4. Ban the person from the project for a time or for good.

The response will match how serious the behavior was and whether it happened before.
